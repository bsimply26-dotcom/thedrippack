FAQ

How does it work

Hook the open sachet over your cup. Pour 40ml just off the boil and wait 30 seconds, then fill to 150ml in stages and lift the sachet out.

What is in the sachet

100% roasted and ground arabica coffee, 10g in every sachet.

How many cups do I get

30 cups in the box, 30 sachets x 10g, net weight 300g e. 12 cups in the trial, 12 sachets x 10g, net weight 120g e.

Where do I buy it

On Amazon.ae. Every buy button on this site goes to the listing.

How do I store it

Keep in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.

What do I need to brew it

A cup and hot water.
